# Mathematics and Statistics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on applying fundamental mathematical and statistical concepts using Python. The objective is to analyze business data using Linear Algebra, Probability, and Descriptive Statistics to identify patterns, understand relationships, and support data-driven decision-making.

The implementation is carried out using Python libraries such as NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, and Statistics in Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook or VS Code.

## Objectives

- Understand and implement Linear Algebra operations using NumPy.
- Apply probability concepts to business scenarios.
- Demonstrate different probability distributions.
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis on business sales data.
- Interpret statistical results to understand data patterns and distributions.

## Technologies and Libraries Used

- Python
- NumPy
- Pandas
- SciPy
- Statistics
- Matplotlib (if used for visualization)
- Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook / VS Code

## Tasks Performed

### Task 1 – Linear Algebra Implementation

**Dataset:** Predefined vectors and matrices.

The following operations are performed using NumPy:

- Vector addition and subtraction.
- Vector dot product.
- Matrix addition.
- Matrix multiplication.
- Calculation of the determinant of a matrix.
- Finding the inverse of a matrix if its determinant is non-zero.
- Computing eigenvalues and eigenvectors.

**Objective:** To understand vector and matrix operations and their applications in mathematical computations.

### Task 2 – Probability Concepts

Probability concepts are applied to customer purchase and sales scenarios.

**Activities performed:**

1. **Probability of Successful Sales**
   - Calculate the probability of successful and unsuccessful sales.

2. **Conditional Probability**
   - Analyze purchase probabilities for Premium and Regular customer segments.
   - Compare customer purchasing behavior based on segments.

3. **Bayes' Theorem**
   - Apply Bayes' theorem to calculate the updated probability that a customer is Premium, given that the customer makes a purchase.

4. **Binomial Distribution**
   - Simulate a binomial distribution using 20 trials and a success probability of 0.65.

5. **Normal Distribution**
   - Generate a normal distribution with a mean of 50 and a standard deviation of 10.

6. **Uniform Distribution**
   - Generate a uniform distribution using a lower limit of 0 and an upper limit of 1.

**Objective:** To understand probability, conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, and probability distributions through business-related examples.

### Task 3 – Descriptive Statistics

**Dataset:** Business Sales Data

The following statistical measures are calculated using NumPy, Pandas, and the Statistics library:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Range
- Variance
- Standard Deviation
- Skewness
- Kurtosis

**Data Interpretation:**

- Analyze the central tendency of sales data.
- Understand the spread and variability of sales values.
- Measure the skewness and kurtosis of the dataset.
- Interpret whether the dataset is symmetric, positively skewed, or negatively skewed.

**Objective:** To summarize and interpret business sales data using descriptive statistical techniques.
